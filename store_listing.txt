APP NAME (30 chars max):
PhonePad – Bluetooth Trackpad

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max):
Turn your phone into a wireless trackpad for your Windows PC. No app on PC needed.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max):
PhonePad turns your Android phone into a precision Bluetooth trackpad for your Windows PC — no companion app, no Wi-Fi, no USB cable. Just pair and go.

Your phone connects directly to your PC as a standard Bluetooth mouse using the HID Device profile. Windows sees it as a native input device, so there's nothing to install on your computer. It works out of the box with any Windows PC that has Bluetooth.

TRACKPAD FEATURES
• Smooth cursor movement with three-tier pointer acceleration
• Tap-to-click for left click
• Two-finger tap for right click
• Two-finger scroll (vertical and horizontal)
• Three-finger gestures for task switching
• Four-finger gestures for desktop management
• Adjustable sensitivity to match your preference
• Natural scroll option (like a MacBook trackpad)

DESIGNED FOR COMFORT
• Full-screen trackpad surface — your entire screen is the touch area
• Immersive mode hides status and navigation bars
• Three dark themes: Dark, Darker, and AMOLED Black
• Haptic feedback on taps, scrolls, and gestures
• Touch ripple effect (optional)

WORKS WITH YOUR SETUP
• Connects via Bluetooth — no Wi-Fi network required
• No companion app or server needed on your PC
• Manages multiple paired PCs with per-device settings
• Rename your devices for quick identification
• Automatic reconnection when your PC comes back in range

BUILT-IN GESTURE GUIDE
• Interactive reference for all supported gestures
• Collapsible sections for movement, clicks, scrolling, and multi-finger gestures
• Always accessible from the trackpad screen

PRIVACY FIRST
• No internet permission — PhonePad never connects to the internet
• No data collection, no analytics, no ads
• All settings stored locally on your device
• Open source

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 9 (Pie) or later
• Windows PC with Bluetooth
• Bluetooth permissions on your phone

Perfect for presentations, couch computing, media PCs, or anytime your mouse isn't within reach.
